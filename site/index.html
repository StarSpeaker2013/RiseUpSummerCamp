Sample Daily Schedule

What a Day at Camp Looks Like

A predictable, balanced rhythm that mixes movement, learning, creativity, and reflection.

  1. 9:30 – 10:00

    Welcome Circle & Icebreaker

    Kick off the day with energizing games and a warm group welcome.

    👋
  2. 10:00 – 10:45

    Featured Workshop

    Guest-led learning: arts & crafts, STEM, AI, cooking, or rocketry — a new adventure each day.

    🌱
  3. 10:45 – 11:00

    Snack & Social Break

    Refuel and recharge with friends.

    🍎
  4. 11:00 – 12:00

    Hands-On Activity

    Creative competitions, physics labs, food activities, and team challenges.

    🎨
  5. 12:00 – 12:30

    Reflection & Wrap-Up

    Share highlights, celebrate wins, and close the day together.

    ⭐
